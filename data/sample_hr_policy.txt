COMPANY HR POLICY HANDBOOK (SAMPLE)
Applicable to all permanent employees across plant and corporate locations.
Version 1.0 — For demonstration purposes only.

SECTION 1: LEAVE POLICY

1.1 Types of Leave
The company provides three categories of paid leave to all permanent employees:
- Casual Leave (CL): Intended for short, planned personal matters. Employees are entitled to
  12 days of casual leave per calendar year, credited at the start of the year. Casual leave
  cannot be carried forward to the next year and lapses on December 31.
- Sick Leave (SL): Intended for illness or medical needs. Employees are entitled to 12 days of
  sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding
  3 consecutive days. Up to 6 unused sick leave days may be carried forward to the next year.
- Earned Leave (EL): Also called privilege leave, accrued at the rate of 1.5 days per month of
  service (18 days per year). Earned leave may be carried forward up to a maximum balance of
  45 days and is encashable at the time of separation, subject to Finance approval.

1.2 Applying for Leave
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 2 working days in advance,
except in cases of medical emergency. Leave exceeding 5 consecutive days requires approval from
both the reporting manager and the department head. Unapproved absence for more than 3 consecutive
days without communication may be treated as a disciplinary matter.

1.3 Leave Encashment
Earned leave in excess of 30 days at the end of a calendar year may be encashed once per year,
subject to a cap of 10 days, upon written request to HR.

SECTION 2: WORK FROM HOME (WFH) POLICY

2.1 Eligibility
Work from home is available to employees in eligible roles (primarily corporate, IT, finance,
and administrative functions). Plant-floor and production roles that require physical presence
are generally not eligible for WFH, except where explicitly approved by the plant head for
documented reasons.

2.2 WFH Allowance
Eligible employees may avail up to 8 days of work-from-home per quarter, in coordination with
their reporting manager. WFH days do not deduct from leave balances but must be marked on the
HR portal in advance. Employees are expected to remain reachable and meet the same productivity
standards as an in-office day.

2.3 Extended WFH
Any WFH request extending beyond 5 consecutive working days requires written approval from the
department head and a documented business or medical justification.

SECTION 3: REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

3.1 Eligible Expenses
The company reimburses reasonable, business-related expenses including local travel for official
work, official communication (mobile/internet where applicable to the role), and pre-approved
training or certification costs. Original receipts or digital invoices are mandatory for all
claims above INR 500.

3.2 Claim Submission
Reimbursement claims must be submitted through the Finance portal within 30 days of the expense
being incurred. Claims submitted after 60 days require special approval from Finance and are not
guaranteed to be processed. Each claim must include the expense category, amount, date, and
supporting document.

3.3 Travel Reimbursement
For official travel, employees are reimbursed as per their grade-based entitlement (economy
travel for standard grades, and business travel for senior leadership on sectors exceeding
6 hours). Local conveyance for official work is reimbursed at actual cost with supporting bills,
or at the company's standard per-kilometer rate where bills are not available.

3.4 Non-Reimbursable Items
Personal expenses, fines or penalties, alcohol, and entertainment expenses unrelated to official
business are not eligible for reimbursement under any circumstance.

SECTION 4: CODE OF CONDUCT AND GRIEVANCE HANDLING

4.1 Grievance Redressal
Employees with a workplace grievance, harassment complaint, or disciplinary concern should raise
the matter directly with HR through the designated grievance channel. Such matters are handled
confidentially by HR and are not resolved through automated systems or general query channels,
in order to ensure a fair, human-reviewed process.

4.2 Disciplinary Matters
Any disciplinary action, show-cause notice, or conduct-related investigation is managed exclusively
by HR in consultation with the relevant department head, following the company's documented
disciplinary procedure.
